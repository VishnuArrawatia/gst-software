How the reconciliation works

              
A practical view of what happens between file upload and the final workpaper.

            

            
          

          

            

              

                

                  1
                  Source records are read
                

                
The tool reads the uploaded Purchase Register, DN/CN and GSTR-2B workbooks and identifies the relevant transaction table or sheets.

                
    
                  
  • Supplier GSTIN & Our GSTIN
    • 
                  
  • Invoice / document number
    • 
                  
  • Document type and date
    • 
                  
  • Taxable value and GST components
    • 
                

              

              

                

                  2
                  Records are compared
                

                
Invoice-level candidates are evaluated using supplier GSTIN, normalised document numbers, document type, dates and configured value/tax tolerances.

                
    
                  
  • Exact / strong matches
    • 
                  
  • Partial or probable relationships
    • 
                  
  • Missing-side records (2B or Books)
    • 
                  
  • Duplicate protection across batches
    • 
                

              

              

                

                  3
                  Exceptions become actionable
                

                
Instead of only showing a match percentage, the result separates records into categories that can be reviewed by accounts and tax teams.

                
    
                  
  • Vendor follow-up & delay notices
    • 
                  
  • Book correction review (Books-only)
    • 
                  
  • GSTR-2B timing checks
    • 
                  
  • Supporting-document review
    • 
                

              

            

            

              Important decision point: GSTR-2B is a static, auto-drafted ITC statement. Reconciliation is a control over differences; it does not by itself establish that every credit is legally eligible. Final ITC decisions should consider the GST law, invoice/supporting documents, reversals and other applicable conditions.

Section 16: Eligibility & Conditions for Input Tax Credit

                
CGST Act, 2017 — Governing conditions before claiming any purchase credit in GSTR-3B

              

              
            

            

              Clause (aa): Must appear in GSTR-2B
              Sec 16(4) Cutoff: 30th November next FY
              Core Rule: Business use + Tax paid to Govt
            

            

              
Sec 16(2)(a)
Valid original Tax Invoice or Debit Note (or RCM self-invoice / Bill of Entry) must be in possession.

              
Sec 16(2)(aa)
Supplier must file invoice in GSTR-1/IFF so it reflects in recipient's auto-drafted GSTR-2B.

              
Sec 16(2)(b) & (c)
Goods or services actually received and tax charged has actually been paid to the Government.

              
Sec 16(4) Time Limit
Last date to claim ITC of FY is 30th November of next FY or Annual Return filing date, whichever is earlier.

            

            

              ERP Action: "Missing in 2B" bills ko Table 4 me hold rakhein aur supplier follow-up karein.
              
            

          


          

            

              

                
Section 17(5): Blocked Credits (Permanently Ineligible ITC)

                
CGST Act, 2017 — Credits disallowed even if invoice appears in GSTR-2B

              

              
            

            

              GSTR-3B Reporting: Table 4(B)(1) Permanent Reversal
              Reclaim Allowed? NO (Permanent)
            

            

              
17(5)(a) Motor Vehicles
Passenger motor vehicles (≤13 seating capacity) blocked unless for resale, passenger transport, or driving training.

              
17(5)(b) Food & Health
Food & beverages, outdoor catering, beauty, club/health/fitness membership & travel benefits for employees.

              
17(5)(c) & (d) Construction
Works contract & materials used for constructing immovable property (except Plant & Machinery).

              
17(5)(h) Lost / Gifts
Goods lost, stolen, destroyed, written off, or distributed as gifts and free samples.

            

            

              ERP Action: Blocked ITC items ko Table 4(B)(1) / "Ineligible" mark karein taaki excess claim na ho.
              
            

          


          

            

              

                
Rule 36(4) & IMS: 100% GSTR-2B Restriction & Documentary Proof

                
CGST Rules, 2017 — No provisional/excess 5%/10% buffer allowed anymore

              

              
            

            

              Max Unmatched Claim: 0% (Strict 100% 2B Match)
              IMS Portal Actions: Accept / Reject / Pending
            

            

              
Strict 2B Matching
Since Jan 2022, ZERO provisional credit is permitted over GSTR-2B. Books-only invoices must wait until supplier files GSTR-1.

              
IMS Workflow
Use IMS Accept (claim in 3B), Pending (defer to future month within 16(4)), or Reject (wrong GSTIN/amount).

            

            

              ERP Action: Probable 2B matches aur Amount Mismatches ko Editor se turant resolve karein.
              
            

          


          

            

              

                
Rule 37: 180-Day Supplier Non-Payment Reversal

                
Second Proviso to Section 16(2) & Rule 37 — Creditor ageing control

              

              
            

            

              Time Limit: 180 Days from Invoice Date
              Interest: 18% p.a. (Sec 50) if unpaid
              Reclaim: Anytime on Payment (No 16(4) bar)
            

            

              
Reversal Trigger
If supplier value + tax remains unpaid after 180 days from invoice date, proportionate ITC must be reversed in Table 4(B)(2).

              
Unlimited Re-availment
On subsequent payment to supplier, re-claim full ITC in Table 4(A)(5) & disclose in Table 4(D)(1) — Sec 16(4) time limit does NOT apply to reclaims.

            

            

              ERP Action: Unpaid bills par Purchase table me Decision "Reclaim Later" mark karke hold track karein.
              
            

          


          

            

              

                
Rule 37A: Reversal When Supplier Fails to File GSTR-3B

                
CGST Rules, 2017 — Safeguard against supplier GSTR-1 filed without GSTR-3B tax payment

              

              
            

            

              Check Date: 30th September next FY
              Reversal Deadline: 30th November next FY
              Interest after 30 Nov: 18% p.a. from 1 Dec
            

            

              
Supplier 3B Check
Even if invoice appeared in GSTR-2B, if supplier hasn't filed GSTR-3B by 30 Sept next FY, buyer must reverse ITC by 30 Nov in Table 4(B)(2).

              
Post-Filing Reclaim
