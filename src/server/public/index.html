GSTR-1, GSTR-2B and the books — reconciled in one place

The books and the GST Portal rarely agree. This system keeps both untouched, reports their totals separately, and explains every invoice-level difference — it never overwrites a return that has already been filed.

  • GSTR-1: draft against filedEvery invoice side by side, and the month's HSN summary against Table 12.
  • GSTR-2B against the purchase registerSupplier-wise ITC matching; books-only and 2B-only invoices stay visible.
  • ITC held on evidenceEligibility, decision, claim month and reversal kept apart, each with who decided and when.
  • P&L reconciled to GSTR-1Turnover and tax agreed across every registration, unit and month.
